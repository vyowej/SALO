PHASE D3 - RIDER APP SHELL, AVAILABILITY, AUTOMATIC DISPATCH (STOP after)
- Driver mode in the mode switcher; mobile-first shell with bottom navigation (Home, Current delivery, Earnings, Profile). Home shows the Online/Offline toggle, zone and shift, today's earnings, and the next best action. A rider can go Online only when active, on shift, and with location permission; the toggle explains why when disabled.
- AUTOMATIC DISPATCH (no job board): when an order reaches Ready (or the configured earlier point), the DispatchService finds riders who are Online, on shift in the restaurant's zone, and without an active delivery; picks the nearest by last known cached location to the restaurant; and assigns inside a transaction that locks the rider row. Each attempt is a delivery_offers row. The rider must acknowledge within rider_ack_seconds (60 by default) on a full-screen "New delivery" card (pickup name and distance, drop-off AREA only, estimated earning, countdown, sound/vibration toggle). Timeout or decline passes the order to the next nearest rider; if the zone has none, widen to the neighboring zone; if still none, notify the restaurant, customer, and admin. One active delivery per rider. If a rider goes offline, is suspended, or loses location mid-delivery, the order returns to dispatch. A scheduler job handles timeouts and retries so nothing waits forever.
- After acknowledgement the rider sees full pickup details and the customer's name, tap-to-call phone, exact address, delivery note, and the order contents.
